## Plain-language terms

- The client owns its code, its diffs and its receipts. C1 gains no rights
  in them by running the pilot.
- The Selo CLI and verifier stay Apache-2.0. Nothing in the pilot paywalls
  checks, `selo verify`, or receipts; the free tier never shrinks.
- A receipt is **evidence about a recorded run**. It is not a certificate,
  not an endorsement of the code, and not a transfer of any IP right.
- C1 may describe the engagement only in anonymised form, and only after
  the client agrees in writing. No client code, paths, or receipt contents
  are published without that approval.
